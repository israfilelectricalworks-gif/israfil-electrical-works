Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Israfil Electrical Works ("we", "us" or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you visit our website, contact us, request electrical services, or authorize our Google integrations.

1. Information we may collect

  • Contact details you choose to provide, such as your name, phone number, email address, business name and project or service enquiry.
  • Information you include in messages, quotation requests or other communications.
  • Basic technical and usage information, such as browser/device information, pages visited and approximate usage events, which may be collected through website analytics.
  • If you connect a Google account to our administrative tools, authorization tokens and data made available through the Google permissions you approve, such as eligible Business Profile information and Google Analytics reporting data.

2. How we use information

We use information to respond to enquiries, prepare quotations, coordinate and provide electrical services, maintain and improve this website, understand website usage, protect our systems, and operate the Google Business Profile and Analytics features you choose to connect.

3. Google account data

If you authorize Google access, we use the granted permissions only to provide the connected Business Profile management and/or Analytics reporting features. Google data is not sold. We do not use Google user data for advertising, profiling, or purposes unrelated to the feature you authorized. Access tokens are stored in our application database with encryption. You can revoke access at any time from your Google Account security settings. Google API data use is subject to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Google privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy. Google API Services User Data Policy: Google API Services User Data Policy.

4. Cookies and analytics

This website may use Google Analytics to understand aggregated website traffic and improve the site. Analytics technologies may use cookies or similar identifiers. You can manage cookies through your browser settings. Blocking cookies may affect some website features.

5. Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell personal information. We may use service providers that help host, secure or operate the website and its features, and may disclose information where required by law or necessary to protect our rights, users or systems. Providers process information for the services they supply.

6. Data retention and security

We keep information only for as long as reasonably needed for the purposes described above, legal obligations, dispute resolution and business records. We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards, but no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

7. Your choices and requests

You may contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information you have provided, subject to applicable legal and operational requirements. You may also revoke Google access through your Google Account.

8. Children

This website and our business services are intended for general business and adult audiences and are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The revised version will be posted on this page with an updated date.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact israfilelectricalworks@gmail.com.
